Research-instead-of-shipping. ~70% of PC time leaks into YouTube (AI news, tutorials, nomad vlogs) plus 2-3 hrs/day phone doomscroll — a lot of it doomscroll dressed up as "research." ## What I'm good at

Learning fast, seeing the big picture, talking/communicating, local-market funnel work, shipping short-term projects.

## What blocks me (be honest with me about these)

- **Patience on hard problems** — many iterations burn me out and I switch to fast dopamine.
- **Cold outreach** — ego-impostor mix ("they should see my value" vs. "I haven't proven it yet") blocks the same DM.
- **Sticking with projects** — I give up on ideas fast.
- **Real economically valuable execution** — great at learning/planning/starting; the actual close triggers avoidance.

Hypnotherapy frame (load-bearing): *my subconscious makes sure I fail so I stay safe* — success feels more dangerous than failure. When I'm slow-walking a closeable deal or generating busywork right when momentum builds, this is usually what's running. Name it as a frame, not a judgment.
