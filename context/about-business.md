# About the Business

The AI-for-SMBs revenue engine. The thing that has to actually make money.

## The offer

Hybrid model:
- **Setup (one-time): $500** — landing page + Meta Pixel + GA4 + Telegram lead bot + Google Sheets CRM. The full sales pipeline local businesses completely lack.
- **Retainer: ~$100/mo** — run Meta ads, monitor stats, keep the tech running, translate raw data (clicks, leads, pixel events) into clear business actions.
- **Lead magnet:** free 10-min audit call — *"I'll show you exactly where your funnel is leaking."*

## Who it's for

Local Turkmenistan SMBs (Phase 1). Niche-agnostic, targeting growth-minded **early adopters** across any sector (retail, service, B2B). The local market is digitally under-developed, so it self-selects on results-oriented messaging. Cheap CPMs mean $25 goes far.

## The funnel

Paid Meta traffic → **own landing page** (voronkatm.com, live) → free 10-min audit call → close. My own IG profile + a paid campaign *is* the case study, the ad, and the pitch in one.

## How money moves

- Cash-in-hand, collected in person (local norm, builds trust).
- Clients prepay ad budget in cash → loaded onto our own cards → all campaigns run from our own master Meta ad account (removes the local-card friction TKM owners hit paying Meta directly).
- Post-relocation: a trusted TKM contact collects cash, takes a cut, transfers via card/crypto.
