# Funnel Core — the spine

**This file is law.** Every other file in `funnel/` must match what's here: the offer, the price, the ICP, the positioning, and the brand voice. When we edit any funnel part (IG, ad, LP, call, delivery) and it drifts from this — I call it out. When we deliberately change strategy, we change it *here first*, then propagate to the stage files.

## The offer (exact wording — keep consistent everywhere)

- **Setup (one-time): $500 + 3 рекламные кампании бесплатно** — full sales pipeline (landing page + Meta Pixel + GA4 + Telegram lead bot + Google Sheets CRM), plus three campaign setups at no extra charge. **This is the only number named before the sale** (revised 2026-07-29).
- **Retainer: ~$100/mo** — run Meta ads, monitor stats, keep the tech running, translate raw data into clear business actions. **Not named upfront.** Introduced after the $500 is paid and the account has enough data to optimise on — the pitch is optimisation, not maintenance. Natural trigger: the third free campaign is used up.
- **Client ad budget: $50/mo minimum** on retainer — client's cash, separate from the retainer, 100% goes to Meta. (The pilot test itself is **$25 / 5 days** — see §Terms.)
- **Lead magnet:** free 10-minute audit call — *"we'll show you exactly where your funnel is leaking."*
- **Price is never shown on the LP or in ads.** The free audit names the price per-business. (Protects the quote; price varies.)

## Terms — two tiers (decided 2026-07-20)

**Pilot — clients 1–7 (revised 2026-07-28). Proof-first: they see value, then decide.**
1. We build the full system **free, in 5 days**.
2. Day-5 meeting: client pays **$25 cash** — their money, 100% to Meta, none to us. We connect their Instagram at that same meeting ([07-client-ig-access](07-client-ig-access.md)). Client shoots creative to our spec, or we reuse what's already on their account; we make the ad.
3. Ads run **5 days**, ~$5/day.
4. **The price is named out loud BEFORE Day 1** — **$500 + 3 free campaign setups. The $100/mo is NOT mentioned.** **No N, no threshold, no guarantee.** After 5 days they see the numbers and decide; yes → $500 cash. The retainer comes later, off the back of real data.
5. Client obligation: answer leads **same-day** during the test (call-tracking bot is the evidence — see [06-delivery](06-delivery.md)).
6. Leverage: LP, bot, CRM run on **our** accounts until the $500 is paid. No pay → pipeline off.
7. Framing on the call: scarcity — «так мы делаем только с первыми клиентами». **Not a guarantee** — there is no threshold to defend, and promising one we didn't agree costs more than the objection.

**In practice it is named on the FIRST call — Operator, 2026-08-08:** «500$ + 3 кампаний i name on the
first call». Not at the confirmation call, not at the meeting. Earlier than this file required, and
that is the standard: there is no client on the board who heard the number for the first time after
the build started.

**Naming the price before Day 1 is the entire conversion mechanism.** The N-hit trigger (pilot v1, 2026-07-20) is gone, so nothing else forces a decision at day 5. If the number first comes up at that meeting, the client already holds a working system and the answer is «подумаю». Said on the confirmation call, the day-5 meeting is a decision; said at the meeting, it's an ambush.

**Why the retainer is held back (2026-07-29):** one number at the close instead of two. «$500 и ещё сто каждый месяц» is a subscription decision for someone who has never bought marketing before; «$500 и три кампании included» is a purchase. The retainer sells far better later, against their own numbers, as optimisation rather than upkeep.

**The risk this creates, and the guard against it:** the retainer is where the actual money is — $500 once versus $1 200 a year. Delaying that conversation is a tactic; skipping it turns this into a one-off-fee business. **The three free campaigns are the trigger, so define them at the sale:** three campaign setups (each = new creative + audience + budget config), **not** counting the 5-day pilot test, valid **3 months**. When the third is spent, that is the retainer conversation, and it arrives on schedule instead of never.

**Standard — client 4+:** 50% ($250) cash upfront → build → 50% ($250) on live. **Turnaround promise: 7 days** (build in 3–4, promise 7).

**Price policy:** $500 is the anchor, named only on the audit call, always paired with the 3 free campaigns so it reads as a package and not a bare fee. Flex down only to close a deal in exchange for a public case study / review; **floor $300**. The $100/mo retainer never flexes — and is never quoted before the $500 is paid.

## Who it's for (ICP)

Local Turkmenistan SMBs, Phase 1. **Niche-agnostic** — any sector (shops, cafes, salons, services, private masters, B2B). Target the growth-minded **early adopters**. The market self-selects on results-oriented messaging. One outcome (clients), any industry — the funnel *is* the niche.

## Positioning

- **Agency, not freelancer.** We are a team — «мы», never «я». Reads bigger and more trustworthy to a business paying for a system.
- **Show the system, not the story.** The repeatable machine is the proof. The case study is one small plain line, never the whole pitch.
- **The page is proof.** "The ad brought you to this page — that's the exact system we build for you."

## Strategy (the one path to revenue)

Paid Meta traffic → **own LP** (voronkatm.com) → free 10-min audit call → close ($500 + 3 free campaigns; retainer later). Own IG profile + a live paid campaign *is* the case study, the ad, and the pitch in one. The machine works end-to-end. **Which stage is the current bottleneck is a status question, not a law** — it lives in [STATE.md](../STATE.md) and is deliberately not stated here.

Money: cash-in-hand, in person. Clients prepay ad budget in cash → our cards → our master Meta account (removes local-card friction). Post-relocation: trusted TKM contact collects + transfers.

## Proof point (ammo — not required on every page)

End-to-end IG funnel for a physical-goods SMB: **~$2 CAC on $100 AOV.** This is real ammo for the **audit call**, **outreach**, and the **IG case-study post** — where a number lands.

**The LP deliberately does NOT put a proof number on the page** — it sells the *system* generically («система, доведённая до звонка»), and that's correct. Don't force a case line onto the LP. If a proof number is ever used in client-facing Russian, say it plainly — *"примерно по паре долларов за клиента, при среднем чеке около 100 долларов"* — never "$2 CAC" (banned jargon).

## Brand voice — CLIENT-FACING (this is NOT The personal voice)

> The personal working voice lives in `references/voice.md`. This section is the **voronka.tm client-facing brand voice** — different thing. Everything a prospect sees.

- **100% Russian UI.** Every visitor-facing word is Russian — headlines, buttons, form fields, placeholders, errors, chatbot lines. Only Latin allowed: brand/proper names (Instagram, Telegram, CRM, voronka.tm).
- **Agency «мы»** — «настроим», «построим», never «я». Address the client as «вы».
- Words a shop owner uses: клиенты, покупатели, заявки, заказы, продажи, реклама, Instagram, Telegram.
- **Banned jargon:** CAC, лиды, конверсия, ROI, performance, трафик — any Western agency-speak.
- **Allowed** (understood locally, signals competence): таргетированная / таргет-реклама, Telegram-бот, CRM-таблица, аналитика.
- Short sentences. Confident, not hypey. Simple but not dumbed-down.
